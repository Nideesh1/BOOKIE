Decoupled harness: edge, bus, brain, store

Public data and the market come in through a thin FastAPI edge. Redis Streams is the only coupling. A FastStream worker turns stream messages into Atlas writes and Hatchet runs. Atlas holds every piece of state. Nothing on the right ever touches the internet.

Rules of the box

  1. Edge never reasons. It talks to the internet and the bus. It reads Atlas only to render.
  2. Brain never touches the internet. It reads the bus and Atlas. Swap NWS for anything and the brain is unchanged.
  3. Bus is transport. Atlas is truth. Streams are trimmed; a replay is a Mongo query, not an xrange.
  4. Hatchet owns time. Durable waits, cron, retries. FastStream owns fan-in. They do not overlap.
  5. Agent reads scores, never writes them. The scorer is code. The agent only rewrites its rulebook.
  6. No order code. The market is the scoreboard. Approval flips a field in Atlas.

Decision 1 · what starts a proposal

Event-driven (drawn): the first wx:forecast for the target day after 15:00 ET starts market_day. Reacts when NWS updates. Alternative: a fixed 16:00 cron in Hatchet. Simpler, but the demo story is weaker.

Decision 2 · processes

One image, three entrypoints: main.py (FastAPI edge), worker.py (FastStream subscribers), brain.py (Hatchet worker). Same pattern as AI_HOSPITAL_BACKEND. Alternative: merge worker and brain into one process. Fewer moving parts, but a slow agent turn then blocks stream consumption.
