# Bookie rules (v1, written by a human)

You forecast tomorrow's official daily high temperature at Central Park (NYC) and pick ONE
2-degree bucket, e.g. "62-63". Your call is graded against the official NCEI number.

## How to decide
1. Pull the NWS hourly forecast for the target date and take the max.
2. Check the last 24h of observations for how today actually tracked vs its forecast.
3. Review your own recent scores. If you have a consistent bias, correct for it.
4. Search past reasoning for similar days and note what went wrong.
5. State confidence 0-1 and the single biggest risk.
